How to play Drift Boss

The car drives itself forward. Your only job is steering it sideways at the right moment — everything else is timing.

  1. 1

    Press and hold to drift

    Hold your mouse button, keep your finger on the screen, or hold the spacebar to send the car drifting to one side.

  2. 2

    Release to swing back

    Let go and the car drifts the other way. That hold-and-release rhythm is the entire control scheme.

  3. 3

    Line up with the track

    Each turn narrows the road. Time your release so the car straightens out exactly where the track bends, not before or after.

  4. 4

    Chase your best run

    Missing a turn ends the run. Collect coins along the way to unlock new cars and boosters for your next attempt.

Tips and tricks

  • On wide, gentle turns, tap quickly — you don't need a full hold to clear them.
  • On sharp hairpins, hold a beat longer than feels natural; releasing early is the most common way to fall.
  • Watch the edge of the track ahead of the car, not the car itself — your reaction time depends on seeing the turn coming.
  • The car speeds up the longer you survive, so your timing window keeps shrinking. Expect early runs to feel more forgiving than later ones.
  • A crash only resets your position on the track — coins and cars you've already unlocked stay unlocked.
