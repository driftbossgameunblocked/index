How to play

  1. 1

    Your car drifts forward on its own — you don't steer the straights.

  2. 2

    Watch for the marker that lights up just before a corner. That's your window.

  3. 3

    Tap, click, or hit space right as it lights up. Too early or too late and you skid off the road.

Why "unblocked"

The whole game is one HTML5 canvas — no plugins, no launcher, no third-party embed to get flagged. It loads on school wifi, office networks, and anywhere else the usual game sites don't. Close the tab, open it again later: your best score is waiting, saved right in this browser.
